About us

Practical software,
built from experience

BezOps Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a South African software company. We build products that solve problems we have lived through ourselves — and we stand behind every one of them.

Our founder

Deon Bezuidenhout

Deon founded BezOps Technologies after a career spanning fintech SaaS, insurance administration and school finance. Over the years he saw the same pattern again and again: good businesses held back by systems that were hard to reach, paperwork that was hard to find, and finance offices running on spreadsheets.

Each BezOps product started as a real problem. EduFAS was built to run the finances of a group of specialist schools. SlipOps grew out of EduFAS, when business people kept asking for its slip-capture feature for their own records. VoiceOps brings it all together — letting people talk to business systems the way they talk to each other.

Deon still works hands-on in every product, which means the person who designs the software is the person you speak to.
