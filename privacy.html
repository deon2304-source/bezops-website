Privacy Policy

How we handle the personal information you share with us through this website, in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

This website, bezops.com, is operated by BezOps Technologies (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa (“BezOps”, “we”, “us”). For the personal information described in this policy, BezOps is the responsible party as defined in POPIA.

Our Information Officer is Deon Bezuidenhout, who can be contacted at info@bezops.com.

2. What information we collect

We only collect the personal information you choose to give us when you use the contact form:

  • your name, and optionally your company name and phone number;
  • your email address;
  • the product you are interested in and the message you write.

To protect the form from spam and abuse, our server briefly uses your internet (IP) address to limit how many messages can be sent from one connection. We do not store it with your enquiry.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics that profile visitors.

3. Why we use it

We use your information only to:

  • reply to your enquiry and send you an acknowledgement that we received it;
  • discuss the products or services you asked about, if you would like to take things further.

We do not add you to a mailing list, and we never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers (operators under POPIA) to run this website and deliver email. They process information only on our instructions:

  • Render — hosts this website and the service that receives the contact form;
  • SMTP2GO — delivers the enquiry and acknowledgement emails;
  • Google Workspace — hosts our company mailbox, where enquiries are received;
  • Google Fonts — supplies the typeface used on this website, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers when a page loads.

Some of these providers store or process information on servers outside South Africa. Where this happens, we rely on providers that are subject to data protection laws or contractual safeguards offering protection comparable to POPIA, as required by section 72 of the Act.

We will only disclose your information to anyone else if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any business relationship that follows, or as long as the law requires. After that, we delete them.

6. How we protect it

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, the contact form accepts messages only from our own website, and access to our mailbox is restricted and protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable, appropriate measures to keep your information safe.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or that we are no longer entitled to keep;
  • object to us processing your information;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, simply email info@bezops.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa):

Website: inforegulator.org.za
Complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za

9. Our products

This policy covers this website only. Personal information processed inside our products (VoiceOps, SlipOps and EduFAS) is handled under the agreements and terms that apply to those services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
